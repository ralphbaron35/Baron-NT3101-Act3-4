About Rimuru Tempest

Nice to meet you. I'm the slime, Rimuru Tempest. I'm not a bad slime, you know.

Rimuru Tempest is the genderless, kind-hearted yet immensely powerful main character of the popular series That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime, who begins as an ordinary Japanese man named Satoru Mikami, gets killed protecting a friend, and is reborn in a magical fantasy world as a seemingly weak slime; despite their humble form, Rimuru quickly gains legendary abilities like the "Great Sage" and "Predator," befriends the ancient dragon Veldora, gathers loyal allies, unites various monster clans, builds the peaceful nation of Jura Tempest Federation, and eventually evolves into a supreme Demon Lord and one of the strongest beings in the world, all while striving to create a place where everyone can live freely and be treated with fairness.
